About

I have spent twenty years building systems where software meets hardware. I started on multifunction printer software and semiconductor factory automation in Bangalore, moved to Singapore to build embedded software for Rohde & Schwarz handheld spectrum analyzers, and joined SLB in 2015.

At SLB I led the architecture for a new generation of flow computers for the VxSpectra multiphase flow meter, from a hardware evaluation across 17+ vendors to a containerized microservices platform, released commercially in 2022, that cut cross-platform integration time by 75%. I also led the VxWave microwave flow meter software and brought augmented reality into our Singapore engineering center for remote client inspections.

Since 2024 I have led control software for hydraulic fracturing pump fleets in Sugar Land, including running two frac fleets from one control system, and prototyped GenAI predictive maintenance and intelligent pump control for them. Outside the day job I'm testing how well small language models answer questions from real equipment manuals on hardware with no network. I write for trade publications, have papers under review at IEEE and SPE journals, and review for IEEE and Elsevier journals.

Long bio · 122 words

Sathappan Alagusundaram is an engineering leader and systems architect at SLB in Sugar Land, Texas. Over twenty years he has built software platforms and products at Toshiba, Novellus Systems, Rohde & Schwarz and SLB, in India, Singapore and the United States. At SLB he led the containerized platform architecture for the VxSpectra flow computers and introduced augmented reality for remote equipment inspection at the company's Singapore technology center. He now leads control software for hydraulic fracturing pump fleets, including GenAI predictive maintenance. He has written for Automation World, has papers under review at IEEE and SPE journals, reviews for IEEE and Elsevier journals, and is an IEEE Senior Member. He holds a B.Tech in electronics and communication engineering from Puducherry Technological University.
